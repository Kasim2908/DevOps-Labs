# 📜 Kubernetes History

## Evolution of Application Deployment

### 1. Physical Servers

Applications were deployed directly on physical machines.

Problems:
- Low resource utilization
- Expensive
- Difficult to scale
- Hardware dependency

---

### 2. Virtual Machines

Hypervisors allowed multiple virtual machines on one server.

Advantages:
- Better utilization
- Isolation

Disadvantages:
- Heavy
- Slow startup
- Multiple operating systems

---

### 3. Containers

Docker introduced lightweight containers.

Advantages:
- Fast startup
- Lightweight
- Portable
- Efficient resource usage

---

## Google Borg

Before Kubernetes, Google developed an internal container management system called Borg.

Borg managed:

- Millions of containers
- Thousands of servers
- Large-scale Google services

Kubernetes was inspired by Borg.

---

## Birth of Kubernetes

- Developed by Google
- Released in 2014
- Open-sourced for the community

Meaning of Kubernetes:

"Kubernetes" is a Greek word meaning **Helmsman** or **Ship Captain**.

---

## CNCF

CNCF stands for:

Cloud Native Computing Foundation

It is responsible for maintaining Kubernetes and many other cloud-native projects.

Examples:

- Kubernetes
- Prometheus
- Helm
- Envoy
- containerd
- Fluentd
